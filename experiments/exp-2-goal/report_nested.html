Why this order: AdaDeduce (backward) derives only the goals' proof chains. Det forward (agent, goal star-order + granular early-stop) derives a bounded cone frontier. DepOnly (batch cone chase + early-stop) materializes whole round frontiers before hitting the goal, so it derives more than Det but less than full Chase. Chase materializes every derivable fact.
