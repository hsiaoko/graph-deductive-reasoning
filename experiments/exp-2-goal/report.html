exp-2-goal · Safe Relevance Filtering (Proposition 2)

BioKG · 720 GARs · single-thread · no cap · dedup derived counts (one chase per config)
Finding: restricting candidate actions to r(g,μ)>0 (the goal cone) removes 98–100% of candidate actions while losing 0 derivable goals — the safe-filtering property holds.

Why safe: the goal cone is closed under premise dependency — a rule whose consequent feeds a cone rule is itself in the cone. So an out-of-cone rule can never contribute to deriving the goal (Theorem 5). Filtering removes only useless candidates.

Caliber note: candidate actions = embeddings_total (pattern matches examined). Unrestricted E_full = 47,280,381. Derived counts here are dedup (one chase), not a per-goal sum — directly comparable across configs.
