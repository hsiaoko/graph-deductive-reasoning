## 二、B：把增益主张立到 hybrid，learned 降为消融

**贡献叙述（建议）**：把「policy-guided deduction」的有效杠杆明确为**证明导向搜索**，而非
动作排序的学习。score-based policy（f）+ early-stop 已是强基线；hybrid 用 backward 的证明导向
性补其浪费，回退保完备。

**Learned policies 小节改写方向（诚实消融）**，可用英文素材：

> We also investigated a learned relevance policy that predicts an action's
> relevance to the goal from successful reasoning chains (target
> \(y=\gamma^{d_C(\mu,g)}\)). Despite training on thousands of proof-chain
> examples, it does not improve over the score-based policy: its best variant
> matches but does not beat Deterministic, and the pure model is slower.
> We identify three reasons. (i) The training chains are generated by the
> score-based policy itself, so the model can at best distill that policy's
> notion of relevance, not exceed it. (ii) The handcrafted relevance term
> \(\gamma^{d(p_0)}\) is predicate-level; it cannot distinguish which ground
> instances of a relevant predicate lie on the proof (instance-level
> proof-membership AUC of the score is 0.55). (iii) Exploratory rollouts do not
> discover substantially cheaper proofs (best 0.82x on lucky seeds), so no
> better-than-teacher training signal is available from exploration.

**机制洞察（可作为 discussion 亮点）**：oracle 上界显示证明锥很小（均值 16 事实）而 forward
探索巨大（均值 46048，deep 层 98% 浪费）——头room 海量，但它是**实例级 brute-force** 浪费，
换排序顺序跨不过；唯有直接识别证明实例（backward 的常量绑定）才能吃到，而 backward 在中心谓词
上反向爆炸。这解释了「为什么学习动作排序无效、而证明导向搜索有效」。

## 三、C（可选）：把 learned 改为策略选择器

**C 已建成实测，是 learned 的实在贡献.** 把 learned 对象从「动作排序」换成「**goal 级搜索策略
选择**」。`LearnedStrategyExecutor` 用 logistic 分类器（cone_size + log-degree → backward 成功，
5 折 CV 准确率 0.853 / AUC 0.893）路由：预测成功→backward+回退，预测失败→直接 forward。
实测 derived 37701 / execution 2.051s / hit 1.0，**vs Det −18.1% / −16.1%**，≈hybrid（决策为
学习而非固定规则）。表述方向：

> Rather than learning to rank individual actions, we learn to select the
> search strategy per goal. A goal-level classifier (dependency-cone size and
> predicate degree; cross-validated AUC 0.89) predicts whether proof-directed
> backward search will succeed, routing the agent to backward with a forward
> fallback when it will, and to forward directly otherwise. This learned
> strategy selector reduces derived facts by 18.1% and execution time by 16.1%
> over the score-based policy while preserving goal completeness, skipping
> futile backward attempts on central predicates.

诚实归因：增益机制与 hybrid 同源（浅 goal 选 backward），学习贡献在于**自适应路由**（AUC 0.89
分类器），而非新的搜索机制。论文宜把 learned policy 定位为策略选择器，动作排序 relevance model
作为消融。

## 四、不要主张

- ❌「learned relevance policy 带来增益」——无证据（封顶 ≈ Det）。
- ❌「HDC 表示优于 numeric」——既有结论边界已否（见实验结论边界）。
- ❌ 把 hybrid 增益归因于学习——hybrid 无任何模型参与。
- ⚠️ 锥过滤收益须限定非中心谓词（中心谓词锥过滤仅 ~11%，早期停止才是主导）。

## 五、已探索并证伪的方向（A，写成诚实负面结果）

实例级价值学习奖金数量级（oracle 98% 浪费），但已用探针证伪其可行性：HDC 余弦与多跳 BFS 图距离
对证明成员身份的 AUC 都只在浅 goal 强（round2/3：0.73–0.92），深 goal（round4，硬 goal）≈随机
（0.44 / 0.54）。深 goal 证明成员身份是规则可派生性，不是基图实体proximity，实例级图特征量错对象。
建议写成「我们探索了实例级学习路线并发现其局限」——头room 真实但非排序学习可得，需结构证明搜索。
这是有价值的诚实发现，比悬置的「未来工作」更扎实。可用英文素材：

> We explored whether instance-level features could identify proof-relevant
> actions on hard goals. Both HDC action-goal similarity and multi-hop graph
> distance separate proof members on shallow goals (AUC 0.73-0.92) but fall to
> chance on deep goals (0.44 / 0.54), where proof membership is governed by
> rule derivability rather than entity proximity. The large oracle headroom
> (98% waste) is thus not reachable by learned action ranking; it requires
> structural proof-directed search, which is what the hybrid executor provides.
